# Microsoft Fundamentals — Interactive CBT

An interactive, adaptive computer-based training course for the **Microsoft Fundamentals (AZ-900 / AI-900 / DP-900 / SC-900)** certification, built by Interstitium Labs.

**Live:**
- https://interstitiumlabs.dev/bootcamps/ms-fundamentals/
- https://learn.interstitiumlabs.dev/bootcamps/ms-fundamentals/
- https://enmanueldmejia.com/bootcamps/ms-fundamentals/

## What's inside

- **32 adaptive scored scenarios** across 4 domains, with a drill queue that targets your weakest areas first
- **4 structured lessons** (concepts, worked examples, common pitfalls)
- **5 guided hands-on labs** + **4 simulated terminal missions** — everything runs in your browser, nothing leaves your device
- **3 multi-step troubleshooting simulations**
- **Timed exam-prep mode**: 30 items in 45 minutes
- **Chat practice companion** for mock-assessment drills

## How it works

Open `index.html` in any browser — self-contained static site, zero dependencies. Progress and mastery are tracked locally in your browser (localStorage).

**Independent practice material.** Not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Microsoft. Confers no certificate. All scenarios are original; no exam content is reproduced.
